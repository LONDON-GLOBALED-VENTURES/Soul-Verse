Delete your SoulVerse account

SoulVerse is published by LONDON GLOBALED VENTURES LTD. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open SoulVerse and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Make sure you're signed in.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Can't use the app?

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the Google account you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address and profile picture from Google sign-in.
  • The prayers you shared in Community Prayers.
  • Your "prayed for" likes on other people's prayers.

What we keep

Nothing linked to you. Favourites, reminders and verse styles are stored only on your phone; uninstalling the app removes them.

Subscriptions: deleting your account doesn't cancel a SoulVerse Premium subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or the App Store (Settings › your name › Subscriptions).
