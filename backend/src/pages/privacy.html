Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the SoulVerse app collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. SoulVerse is published by LONDON GLOBALED VENTURES LTD, a company registered in England and Wales ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for your personal data.

Questions or requests: email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

The short version

  • You can read verses, use templates, set reminders and save favourites without an account. That data stays on your phone.
  • If you sign in with Google, we store your name, email address and profile picture so you can share prayers and subscribe to Premium.
  • Prayers you share are shown to other SoulVerse users. Post anonymously if you don't want your name shown.
  • Photos you edit stay on your phone. We never upload them.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can delete your account, and the data linked to it, at any time in the app.

1. What we collect

When you sign in with Google

Your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID, which Google shares with us when you sign in. We also record when your account was created and when you last signed in.

When you use Community Prayers

  • Prayers you share: the title, text, category and type (request or praise), and whether you posted anonymously. Unless you post anonymously, your name and profile picture are shown with the prayer.
  • "Prayed for" taps: which prayers you prayed for, and counts of prayers shared and received.
  • Reports: when you report a prayer, the reason and any details you write. The person you report isn't told who reported them.
  • Blocks: the people you've blocked, so their prayers are hidden from you.

Prayers can reveal your religious beliefs, which data protection law treats as sensitive. Only share what you're comfortable with other users seeing, and use the anonymous option if you prefer. Choosing to post a prayer to the community is choosing to make it visible to other users.

When you subscribe to Premium

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your card or bank details. We receive the status of your subscription (for example active, cancelled or expired), its renewal or expiry date, and store transaction identifiers, through our subscription provider RevenueCat.

Technical data

Like most online services, our servers record basic request information (such as IP address, time, the address requested and app or browser type) to keep the service running securely and fix problems.

Stored only on your phone

Favourites, reminder times, your chosen moods, verse styles, home-screen widget content, the community guidelines agreement and any photos you pick for the editor are kept on your device. We don't receive them. Uninstalling the app removes them.

3. Who we share it with

  • Other SoulVerse users see prayers you share, with your name and picture unless you post anonymously.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contract:
    • Google: Google Sign-In, and Google Cloud, which hosts our server (Belgium, EU).
    • Neon: our database (London, UK).
    • RevenueCat: subscription management (United States).
    • Google Play and Apple: purchases and subscriptions, under their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.
  • A buyer or successor, if SoulVerse or our company is sold or reorganised; this policy would continue to apply to your data.

We don't sell or rent your personal data.

4. International transfers

Some providers, such as RevenueCat and Google, may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account until you delete it. Deleting it removes your profile, the prayers you shared, your "prayed for" taps, your reports and your blocks straight away.
  • A prayer until you delete it, delete your account, or we remove it for breaking our rules.
  • Backups of the database are kept for a short time and are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Server logs for up to 30 days.
  • Subscription records held by RevenueCat, Google Play or Apple are kept under their own policies, for example for tax and accounting.
  • Emails you send us for as long as needed to deal with them.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or give it to you in a portable format. To delete your account, use Profile › Delete account in the app, or see our account deletion page. For anything else, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please contact us first. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or your local data protection authority.

7. Children

SoulVerse is not for children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, email us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored with providers that encrypt it at rest. Sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and limit who can access it.

9. Permissions on your phone

  • Notifications: for the daily verse reminders you choose.
  • Photos: to pick a photo as a background, and to save the images you create. Photos stay on your phone.

You can turn these off at any time in your phone's settings.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
